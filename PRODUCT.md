## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** locals in Lafayette and the Lamorinda area (Orinda, Moraga, Walnut Creek) deciding where to get espresso, gelato or a pastry, often on a phone, often walking past Plaza Way.
- **At the table:** guests already seated inside or on the patio who scan a QR code to read the menu instead of a paper one.
- **After the visit:** happy guests who scan a second QR code to leave a Yelp review.
- **The person this is pitched to:** Castagna's owner. Arman Digital builds this as an unsolicited demo from public information to win them as a client, so nothing on it may read as an official statement the owner hasn't made.

## Product Purpose

A website for Castagna Pasticceria that makes a small, unclaimed, word-of-mouth shop findable and desirable. It answers when they're open, what's in the case and where to sit, and it ships two working tools a small café rarely has: a phone-first menu opened by a table QR code, and a review QR code that sends guests straight to Yelp's review form. Success means a visitor walks over, and the owner sees a site worth paying for.

## Positioning

An Italian-owned pasticceria, gelateria and espresso bar where the pastries, cookies and breads are baked on site, "almost in front of you," and the gelato is made with real flavors (sour cherry, ricotta, cassis, spumoni, tiramisu). Reviewers compare it to a small European café: you choose from what was made that day, and staff bring your order to your table.

## Operating Context

- Address: 3531 Plaza Way, Lafayette, CA 94549 (Lafayette town center, patio seating on the plaza).
- Hours (Yelp, Oct 2026): Mon closed · Tue–Thu 9:00 AM–3:00 PM · Fri–Sat 8:30 AM–4:00 PM · Sun 9:00 AM–3:00 PM.
- Amenities on Yelp: validated parking, dogs allowed. Reviews add indoor and outdoor seating and table service.
- No phone number or website is listed anywhere public. The Yelp listing is unclaimed.
- The menu changes daily ("choose what's available that day").

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home page plus a `/menu` page built for a phone held at a table, and the two QR codes (menu and review).
- The review QR targets Yelp's write-a-review URL: `https://www.yelp.com/writeareview/biz/UQmPkSxEbMG9AJ-aWmnOVQ`.
- **Prices:** only these are confirmed, read off the shop's own signage in customer photos: gelato small $4.75, medium $6.75, large $8.00; toppings 60¢; croissant $3.50; bostock (hazelnut, tropical) $3.50; mixed berry scone $4.75. Every other price is unknown and must read "price at the counter" until Arman supplies the menu board. Never invent a price.
- Coffee syrups (chalkboard): vanilla, hazelnut, salted caramel, peppermint, almond, lavender, chocolate; sugar-free available for some. Milk: whole, 2%, non-fat, oat, almond, soy.
- Items confirmed by reviews or photos: espresso, cortado, latte (iced), affogato, gelato, croissant, chocolate almond croissant, bostock, mixed berry scone, brownie, cookies, cannoli, sfogliatelle (added later), focaccia sandwiches, panini, focaccia pizza, sourdough boule, breads, Sanpellegrino sodas, teas.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: "Castagna Pasticceria". The shop window shows "CASTAGNA" in arched capitals over "Pasticceria" in script, plus "Espresso" and "Panini" neon. A "Gelato" neon script sign hangs inside.
- Observed identity in the shop: red cups and red saucers, vintage Italian posters (Puccini's Turandot, Martini & Rossi), navy-and-white woven bistro chairs, mosaic and marble tables, copper pots, wood and black steel shelving.
- Do not mention La Chataigne or any other business connection (one unverified review only).

## Evidence on Hand

- Yelp: 4.6 stars, 13 reviews, captured October 2026. Positive quotes are usable verbatim with first name and last initial. One negative review exists (missing Italian classics); never imply every review is five stars.
- 14 customer-uploaded Yelp photos in `assets-inbox/yelp/`, used with Arman's approval. The `shop` photo shows a staff member; keep people out of hero use.
- AI slow-motion clips animated from four of those photos (OpenArt, Kling 3.0): affogato pour, cortado pour, croissant pulled apart, gelato scoop. Present them as atmosphere, not documentary footage.
- Absent: owner names, founding story, phone, email, website, social accounts, full menu with prices, the owner's own photography. Do not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. Truth over polish: every fact on the page comes from Yelp, the reviews or the shop's own signage. Gaps are marked, not filled.
2. The phone at the table is the hardest test. The QR menu must open instantly and be readable with one thumb in daylight on the patio.
3. Sell the case, not the café cliché: the specific flavors, pastries and red cups this shop has, not generic coffee imagery.
4. Every surface should make the owner want it, including the printable table card that holds both QR codes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Menu text must stay legible outdoors in sun (high contrast, large type) and work with reduced motion; QR codes always have a tappable link beside them for people already on the site.
